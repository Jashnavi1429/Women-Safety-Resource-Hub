Safety Tips

Simple habits and awareness can help you stay safer in everyday situations.

🚶‍♀️

Personal Safety

  • Stay aware of your surroundings.
  • Prefer well-lit and populated areas.
  • Keep important contacts easily available.
  • Tell a trusted person when you need help.
  • Move to a safe place if you feel uncomfortable.
🚗

Travel Safety

  • Plan your route before travelling.
  • Keep your phone charged.
  • Share travel details with someone you trust.
  • Use trusted transportation where possible.
  • Avoid isolated places when possible.
🔐

Online Safety

  • Use strong and private passwords.
  • Do not share sensitive personal information.
  • Be careful with unknown links and messages.
  • Review privacy settings on social media.
  • Report harmful or suspicious activity.
📱

Emergency Preparedness

  • Keep emergency numbers saved.
  • Keep your phone charged.
  • Know nearby safe locations.
  • Keep trusted contacts informed when needed.
  • Know how to contact local emergency services.
💡

Remember

Your safety comes first. If you feel unsafe, seek help from a trusted person or appropriate emergency service.
